I. The Districts

Where the transformers are.

Ernakulam dominates by every measure — most DTRs, most sections, most installed solar capacity, most transformers already saturated. Wayanad sits at the opposite extreme. Below, each bar shows total DTR capacity (light) overlaid with grid-connected solar (dark blue).

II. Notable Findings

Headroom everywhere, except where it isn't.

The state has used less than a fifth of what its low-voltage grid could absorb. But the distribution is anything but uniform — pockets of saturation sit alongside vast unused capacity.

Most Saturated District
—
DTRs at full capacity in Ernakulam — over four times the next-highest district. The Kochi metro grid is hitting its limits.
Largest Single Section
—
Distribution transformers in Thrikkakkara, Ernakulam — by far the largest section, larger than 13 entire districts' average.
Untouched Inventory
—
DTRs across the state with zero solar connected — roughly one in three. The dormant majority of the network.
